# Customer Dashboard Client Demo Script

## Two-Minute Walkthrough

1. Sign in and open the household overview.
2. Explain that the large live metric is the latest aggregate household power
   from simulated connected devices.
3. Point out the measured, estimated, and simulated labels.
4. Open Energy analytics and show the smart-plug, EV-charger, and heat-pump
   contributions.
5. Open Connected devices and show current state, last seen, flexibility, and
   the simulation badge.
6. Open Flexibility and follow an event from opportunity through simulated
   completion.
7. Open Community and explain the five-household privacy threshold.
8. Open Reports and export a household-scoped CSV.
9. Return to the overview and show a validated AI-powered energy insight.
10. Close with the safety notice: no physical household device is controlled.

## Five-Minute Walkthrough

Add these details:

- switch between authorized pseudonymized households as an operator
- compare the 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day analytics ranges
- explain why heat-pump energy may be estimated from sampled power
- show the flexibility-score component breakdown
- use the existing approval workflow from the customer-friendly event view
- print a PDF-ready report
- sign in as a technical administrator and open `/admin/operations` to
  demonstrate the product/operations separation

## Client-Safe Statements

- The dashboard uses real records from the local simulated pipeline.
- Insights are selected by local AI from aggregate facts and validated before
  display.
- Community data is anonymized and aggregated.
- IEEE 2030.5 and dataspace capabilities are foundations, not certification
  claims.
- Device control remains simulated.

## Statements To Avoid

- guaranteed savings
- carbon reduction without carbon-intensity data
- certified protocol or dataspace compliance
- production identity or cloud deployment already completed
- physical device response
